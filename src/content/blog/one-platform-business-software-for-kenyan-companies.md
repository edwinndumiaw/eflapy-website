One Platform is the business software built and run by Eflapy Digital, an AI operations firm based in Nairobi. It puts the work a company does to win and keep customers into one system: sales, marketing, customer communication, reputation, automation, payments and AI employees. From November 2026, One Platform is available as a desktop app that installs on Windows and macOS computers. A mobile app is in development.

This article explains what One Platform is, what each part does, who it is built for, what it costs, and how a business gets started.

## Why we built One Platform

Most Kenyan companies we work with run their customer work across many separate tools. Enquiries arrive by phone, email, SMS, social media messages and walk ins. Each one is handled by whoever happens to see it first. Sales follow up lives in someone's head or notebook. Marketing is done in one tool, payments are confirmed in another, and reviews are rarely asked for at all.

The result is the same in almost every company: nobody can see the full picture of a customer, management cannot tell how many enquiries came in last month or how many became sales, and the business depends on a few people remembering everything.

We built One Platform so that a company can run all of that work from one place, with one record for every customer and one view for management. The name says what it is: one platform for the whole customer side of a business.

## What One Platform does

One Platform is organised around the work a team does every day. Each part connects to the same customer database, so information entered in one place shows up everywhere it is needed.

### 1. One inbox for every conversation

Messages from email, SMS, WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram and the chat on your website arrive in a single inbox. Every member of the team sees the same conversations, can assign them to a colleague, and can reply from the same screen. When a customer writes on one channel and calls back on another, the full history sits in one place.

### 2. A database of every customer

Every person who contacts your business gets a profile: their details, every message sent and received, their appointments, payments, notes from your team and where they came from. You can tag and group customers, search them, and filter them for follow up or marketing.

### 3. Sales pipelines

One Platform shows your sales as pipelines: stages your deals move through, from first enquiry to closed sale. A real estate company might use enquiry, site visit, reservation, deposit and handover. An insurance broker might use quote requested, quote sent, documents received and policy issued. Each business sets its own stages, and management can see at a glance how many deals sit at each stage and their value.

### 4. Calendars and bookings

Customers can book appointments, site visits, consultations or calls through a booking link or a form on your website. Bookings land in your team's calendars, confirmations and reminders go out automatically, and the booking is attached to the customer's profile.

### 5. Marketing

From the same system, a business can send email and SMS campaigns to the right group of customers, build landing pages and forms that feed new leads straight into the database, and plan and schedule social media posts across its accounts. Because campaigns go to customers already in the database, results can be traced back to actual enquiries and sales.

### 6. Payments

One Platform can send invoices and payment links and record payments against the customer they belong to. For Kenyan businesses it connects with Pesapal, so customers can pay by Mpesa and card. For businesses selling abroad, it connects with Stripe and other international payment providers. Payment status is visible in the customer's profile and can trigger the next step, such as a receipt or a welcome message.

### 7. Reputation management

After a sale, a visit or a completed job, One Platform can send the customer a request to leave a Google review. New reviews are shown in one place so the team can reply to them on time. For many Kenyan businesses, reviews are now the first thing a new customer checks, and this is how a company builds them steadily rather than hoping customers remember.

### 8. Automation

Repetitive work can be set up to run on its own. Examples include replying to a new enquiry within a minute, moving a deal to the next stage when a payment arrives, reminding a customer of an unpaid invoice, sending a follow up three days after a quote, or alerting a manager when a high value lead comes in. Each business decides which steps run automatically and which stay with its people.

### 9. AI employees

One Platform includes AI employees that work inside the system alongside your team. They can answer customer questions on chat, SMS and messaging channels using your company's own information, qualify new enquiries, book appointments into your calendars and hand a conversation to a person when it needs one. Every conversation they have is recorded in the customer's profile, so your team always knows what was said. Eflapy Digital sets up and trains these AI employees for each business as part of our AI operations work.

### 10. Reporting

Management gets dashboards that show enquiries, where they came from, how quickly the team responded, deals by stage, appointments booked, payments received and reviews collected. This is the view most business owners tell us they do not have today.
