**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**NAIROBI, Kenya, 13 November 2026.** Eflapy Digital, an AI operations firm based in Nairobi, today released One Platform as a desktop app for Windows and macOS. One Platform is business software that lets Kenyan companies run their sales, marketing, customer communication, reputation, automation, payments and AI employees from a single system. Businesses can now install it on their computers and work from it as a standalone application, without opening a web browser. A mobile app is in development and will follow.

### One system instead of many

Many Kenyan businesses handle customers across separate tools: phone calls, email, SMS, social media messages, notebooks and payment confirmations checked one by one. One Platform brings these into one place. Messages from email, SMS, WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram and website chat arrive in a shared inbox. Every customer has a single profile holding their conversations, appointments, payments and notes. Sales move through pipelines that each business defines for itself, and management can see enquiries, sales, payments and reviews on one dashboard.

The platform connects with Pesapal so Kenyan customers can pay by Mpesa and card, and with Stripe and other providers for international payments. It sends review requests after each sale or service, runs automated follow ups and reminders, and includes AI employees that answer customer questions, qualify enquiries and book appointments using each company's own information, handing over to staff when a person is needed.

### Quote

"Most of the companies we speak to are not short of customers. They are short of a way to see and handle all of them in one place," said Edwin Ndumia, Head of AI Operations at Eflapy Digital. "One Platform gives a business one system for every conversation, every sale and every payment, and putting it on the desktop means teams can keep it open all day as part of how they work. The mobile app is next."

### Availability and pricing

One Platform is available now to businesses in Kenya and internationally. Each account is set up by Eflapy Digital for the business that uses it, including pipelines, bookings, automations, payment connections and staff training. Setup takes 10 to 14 days.

Pricing in Kenya is KES 30,000 for setup and KES 4,000 per month. Outside Kenya, pricing is USD 400 for setup and USD 97 per month. AI employees and custom work are priced separately.

Eflapy Digital is setting up One Platform first for companies in real estate, insurance, logistics, travel and tourism, legal services, immigration consultancy, and security and facilities management.
